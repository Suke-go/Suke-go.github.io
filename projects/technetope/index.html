Technetope

A swarm of small mobile speakers that treats visitors as part of its environment

Period
2025
Team
Jumpei Saito, Kosuke Shimizu, Tsubasa Sato
Links
  • Project Site
  • GitHub
  • YouTube

Technetope is an installation made with Jumpei Saito and Tsubasa Sato. In a 2.4 m square area, 30 small white cube robots move as a flock. Each is a modified toio robot carrying a small speaker module, so the flock is also a set of moving sound sources. The piece is conceived as an ecosystem built from technology, with visitors as part of its environment.

Each robot refers to its own position, the positions of the others, and the coordinates of people in the space. When a person enters, the flock treats them as an obstacle: it avoids them, splits into smaller groups, and merges again when the groups come close. The behavior is modeled on schools of fish, swarms of locusts and flocks of ducks.

The sound modules are M5StickC Plus2 devices with speaker hats. A PC aligns their clocks over NTP and sends playback commands stamped with a future time over OSC, so that robots spread across the floor can play sounds together. The visuals come in four themes: medaka and a lake, locusts and grassland, ducks and an autumn sky, and stars at night.
