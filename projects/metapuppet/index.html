MetaPuppet

Operating your own body like a marionette

Period
2026
Threads
T1Somatosensory Stimulation Interfaces
Links
  • Project Site

MetaPuppet is a VR experience in which the operator and the operated are the same person. A stereo camera films the participant, the person is cut out of the image in real time, and this live self-image is shown in VR. Cables attached to the wrists are wound by motors, so input from the participant's own hands lifts their own arms. It was made with Anji Fujiwara for IVRC 2026.

The experience is a rhythm game played by raising and lowering the hands to targets. The task stays the same while the view changes in three stages: first person, a third-person doll that copies the participant's pose, and finally the participant's own camera image. Scoring and the cables use only the headset's hand tracking; the camera image and pose estimation are used for display.

Video travels from a Raspberry Pi stereo camera to the PC over a custom UDP protocol, and person matting (Robust Video Matting) and pose estimation run on the GPU. A VRST 2026 poster with Keigo Matsumoto presents the cable-driven device as a tool for studying how agency and body ownership change across visual perspectives.
