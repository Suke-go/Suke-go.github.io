The Stethoscope Device

A 3D-printed holder embeds a small microphone in the chest piece of a double-head stethoscope (FOCAL), and the whole assembly sits inside a stuffed animal. Participants simply hold it to their chest, and the contact stays stable.

The microphone signal travels over a Hollyland Lark A1 wireless link. Sony MDR-CD900ST headphones and a Dayton Audio Bass Shaker return each person's own and the partner's heartbeat as sound and vibration. Analysis and visuals run in TouchDesigner on a Mac mini.

The signal is band-limited to the heart-sound range (about 20–200 Hz), rectified, and low-passed into an envelope. Peaks above a threshold, thinned by a 200–300 ms refractory period, become beat times, from which inter-beat intervals and instantaneous heart rate follow.

Session

  1. 30 sEach person hears their own heartbeat and feels a low vibration synchronized to it.
  2. 1 minThey swap: each hears the other's heartbeat, while the vibration keeps their own rhythm.
  3. 3 minBoth heartbeats shallowly modulate a broadband white-noise field so neither comes too far forward, and the figure deforms with the synchrony index. It has three layers: a distorted outline for divergence, an inner flow for uneven influence, and an afterimage for the history of the relation.
