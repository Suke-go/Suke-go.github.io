MEETME

Meeting a generated double that sends you a video message

Period
2024
Team
Shota Aso, Kosuke Shimizu
Links
  • LAC Activity Report

An installation exhibited at Niconico Chokaigi 2024. Visitors meet a double generated from their own image and voice, who delivers a video message to them, and reflect on themselves through that exchange.

Co-created with Shota Aso and supported by LAC Co., Ltd. through its Sugoude program.
